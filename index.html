Brand guidelines · v0.1

Focus on your art. We handle the rest.

The visual and verbal framework of Aubuscule: a dot-trame mark, a purple palette, two typefaces, a direct voice. Read before producing anything.

01 · Brand

Who we are

Aubuscule is a pair of digital craftspeople based in Brussels. We support artists on four fronts: digital, audio, video, admin. We also publish a few simple pieces of software: Remplate, Zones Tonales.

01SimpleOne need, one clear solution. No jargon, no pointless options.
02CraftedSmall team, careful work, direct contact with both founders.
03Handed overWe build, then hand you the keys: you stay independent.

07 · Voice

Direct, calm, jargon-free

Formal addressThe reader is “you”; the studio is “we”. In French, always vouvoiement.
Benefit firstLead with what the reader gains, then explain how.
Short sentencesOne idea per sentence. No decorative metaphors.
Sentence caseHeadings and buttons: initial capital only. Eyebrows in mono capitals.
We write“We reply fast, no jargon.”
“Fixed price, no subscription.”
We avoid“Innovative, synergistic solutions”
Exclamation marks, emojis, empty buzzwords.
